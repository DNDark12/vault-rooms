# Roadmap

Possible next steps for the secure mesh fork, roughly in order of usefulness:

- **Sub-folder permissions inside a room.** Today a room is the unit of access; a sub-folder with different access is its own room. Separate keys per sub-folder would allow finer rooms.
- **Larger files**: notes above about 6 MB and other files above about 32 MB, sent in parts.
- **History view** of a note's changes and who made them (the signed history is already there).
- **Mobile**, which would need a transport that works on iOS and Android.
